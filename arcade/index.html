Two original games · free · nothing to install

Nobody asked me
to build these.

Which is rather the point. I write games for the same reason I hand-code websites — because the only way to know what a browser can really do is to keep pushing until it complains. Both of these run on your phone.

Prism Runner

SCORE 0DISTANCE 0mBEST 0

Two realities. One runner.

Only the platforms glowing in your current reality are solid. The others are ghosts. Shift between them — including in mid-air — to land on ground that was not there when you jumped.

Desktop: space or up arrow to jump, shift or down arrow to change reality. Phone: tap the left half of the screen to jump, the right half to shift, or use the buttons. Gold platforms exist in both realities and are always safe. Prisms only count while you are in their reality.

Zap The Competition

SCORE 0LIVES 3BEST 0

They are crowding your listing.

Cheap SEO shops, page builders and guaranteed-number-one merchants are descending on your search results. Clear them before they reach the bottom. It is the most cathartic thing on this website.

Desktop: arrow keys to move, F or enter to fire. Phone: drag anywhere on the screen to move and tap to fire, or use the buttons. Each wave gets a little faster. Three make it past you and it is over.

Why a marketing consultant has an arcade

Because the demo is the argument.

Anybody can write “we build engaging interactive experiences” on a services page. Very few can hand you one and let you play it on the bus. Both of these are a few hundred lines of hand-written JavaScript and a canvas — no engine, no plugin, no download — which is exactly the same toolkit behind the quoting engines, configurators and 3D worlds I build for clients who need people to stay on a page.

If the thing that would set your business apart is something nobody in your industry has bothered to build, that is usually the conversation I enjoy most.
