About these tools

A scripted sales coach — rule-based, no AI, nothing sent anywhere — trained on the playbook I used running sales teams for multi-million-dollar plumbing and remodeling companies: tie-downs, trial closes, price conditioning, objection handling, discovery, closing and follow-up. Ask it by topic or in the customer's words.

Tie-downs are small agreement questions at the end of a statement ("…isn't it?") that build momentum. Trial closes ask for an opinion instead of a decision ("how would that change things for you?") so you learn where the buyer is before you ask for the order. Coach Z has examples of both.

Making sure the buyer hears the range before the number — anchored to the cost of the problem — so the price never shocks. The Price-Conditioning tool builds a script from your numbers.

My Sales Help is the full training site: a 201-objection engine, a glossary, games and a course. This page is the working toolkit — the pieces you use on a live call.

Coach Z is scripted, not a large language model, which is why it never invents an answer. If you want an actual AI-powered assistant on your site — one that qualifies, books and hands off to you — that's what I build: sales-trained chatbots.
