Zachary Tye Wennstedt was born in Fremont, Nebraska — and raised on the front range of beautiful Colorado. His story doesn't start with privilege. It starts with pain.

His first memory in life is losing his baby sister, Jasmine, to SIDS at just five months old. He was five years old. That loss planted the seed of something fierce in him — an awareness of how short and precious life truly is.

What followed wasn't easy. Abuse. Trauma. Drugs at eleven years old — before his brain had even fully formed. The kind of start that swallows most people whole. But Zach was not most people.

Through hundreds of failures and the unshakeable grace of God, Zach overcame every addiction, every obstacle, every chapter that was supposed to end him. He didn't just survive. He chose to build something extraordinary from the rubble.

Today he is a CEO, a developer, a strategist — and above all, a father. Being a dad is his greatest achievement. Because he knows what it means to grow up without a present father, he decided to be fiercely present for his own children. Leading by example. Loving completely.

Faith drives everything. Money is just a tool. His real passion is helping others discover what they're truly capable of. Raising the standard — for himself, for his family, and for every person he encounters.

No more hollow words. No more settling. Every step forward is intentional. Every word spoken carries weight. This is a man who earned his voice.
