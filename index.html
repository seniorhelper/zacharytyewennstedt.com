Zachary Tye Wennstedt was born in Fremont, Nebraska — and raised on the front range of beautiful Colorado. His story doesn't start with privilege. It starts with pain.

His first memory in life is losing his baby sister, Jasmine, to SIDS at just five months old. He was five years old. That loss planted the seed of something fierce in him — an awareness of how short and precious life truly is. It never left him.

What followed wasn't easy. Abuse. Trauma. Drugs at eleven years old — before his brain had even fully formed. The kind of start that swallows most people whole. But Zach was not most people. He never was.

Through hundreds of failures and the unshakeable grace of God, Zach overcame every addiction, every obstacle, every chapter that was supposed to end him. He didn't just survive — he chose to build something extraordinary from the rubble. He chose life over everything else.

He chose his children. Being a dad is his greatest achievement and his highest calling. Because he knows what it means to grow up without a present, loving father — he decided to be fiercely present, unconditionally loving, and endlessly intentional for his own kids. Leading by example isn't a strategy. It's a way of life.

Faith drives everything. Not religion — faith. The kind that gets you up at 3am when everything is broken, when the business is struggling, when the weight feels impossible. The kind that says: keep building, keep loving, keep going.

Money is just a tool. His real passion is helping others discover what they're truly capable of — watching people transform their businesses, their thinking, their lives. That's what gets him up in the morning. That's what he was built for.

Today Zach is a CEO, a developer, a strategist, an artist, a poet, a humanitarian, and above all — a father and a man of faith. No more hollow words. No more settling. Every step forward is intentional. Every word spoken carries weight. This is a man who earned his voice — and he's using it to help you build yours.
