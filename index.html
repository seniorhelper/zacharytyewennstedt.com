The Beginning

Zachary Tye Wennstedt was born in Fremont, Nebraska and raised on Colorado’s Front Range. His story does not begin with privilege. It begins with loss — the kind that brands itself permanently into the architecture of a soul.

“I didn’t choose addiction. Addiction chose me. What I chose was to fight — every single day after.”

Zach failed. Repeatedly. Publicly and privately. Business ideas that went nowhere. Partnerships that fell apart. Relationships strained by the weight of a man still becoming who he was meant to be.

The Choice That Changed Everything

And every single time, he got back up. Not gracefully. Not immediately. Not always with a plan. But he got up. Because Jasmine never got the chance to. Because his children deserved a father who modeled what resilience actually looks like.

The addiction was the longest and hardest war. But the day came when the decision was final and absolute. Faith is not Zach’s religion. It is his architecture.
