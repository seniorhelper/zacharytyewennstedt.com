The Beginning — Before the Business

Zachary Tye Wennstedt was born in Fremont, Nebraska and raised on Colorado's Front Range. His story doesn’t begin with a silver spoon. It begins with a siren.

His first memory — the kind that brands itself forever into the architecture of a soul — is losing his baby sister Jasmine to SIDS when he was just five years old. She was five months old. One moment she was there. The next, she wasn’t. That kind of loss doesn’t leave. It becomes the bedrock of everything you either collapse into or build upon. Zach chose to build.

What followed was not a childhood of healing. It was a childhood of survival. Beaten. Molested. Drugged. Not at sixteen. Not at fourteen. At eleven years old, before his brain had fully formed, substances entered his world. By twelve, he was an addict. The kind of start that writes the ending for most people. But Zach was not most people.

“I didn’t choose addiction. Addiction chose me — because the pain was bigger than my ability to carry it. What I chose was to fight. Every single day after.”— Zachary Tye Wennstedt

The Long War — Failure After Failure

The road from rock bottom to building an empire isn’t a montage. It’s years. It’s dozens of failed attempts. It’s starting something and watching it collapse. It’s debt and doubt and the particular darkness that comes at 3am when you don’t know if tomorrow will be better.

Zach hit walls most people never encounter. He hit them repeatedly. Business after business. Relationship after relationship. He failed publicly. He failed privately. And every single time — he got back up. Not gracefully. Not immediately. But he got back up.

The addiction was the longest battle. Years of fighting against a brain rewired by trauma and substances. Until the day came when the decision was final. When he chose freedom — over everything. Freedom from substances. Freedom from the story trauma had written for him. Freedom to become the man he was built to be.

“I used to think my story was my weakness. I was wrong. My story is my greatest credential. Because I know what it means to have nothing — and I know exactly how to build from it.”— Zachary Tye Wennstedt

The Calling — Father, Faith & Purpose

Ask Zach what his greatest achievement is, and he won’t list a business. He will tell you: being a father. Because he knows what it means to grow up without a present, intentional, sober father. He chose to be the opposite — fiercely present, unconditionally loving, leading by example every single day.

Faith is not Zach’s religion. It’s his architecture. The foundation everything is built on. The voice that said keep going when logic said quit. The presence that got him through the nights that had no light.

Today Zach channels his overcomer’s heart into humanitarian and philanthropic work. Helping people discover their capacity. Mentoring others through their darkest chapters. Building businesses that don’t just generate revenue — they generate dignity and opportunity.

The Mission — Building What Matters

Eye To Ad Media. Search Converts. Aging Safely Baths. Showers4Less. A portfolio of digital properties across the USA and Dominican Republic. These aren’t trophies. They are tools. Tools he uses to fund the bigger mission: leaving every person he encounters better than he found them.

He is a CEO, a full-stack developer, a strategist, a poet, an artist, a humanitarian, and above all — a man who earned his voice. Not through ease. Not through luck. Through loss, failure, grace, faith, and the kind of resilience that only gets forged in the hottest fire.

He’s using that voice to help you. To build your business. To generate your leads. To train your team. To take whatever you have — however broken it might feel — and build something extraordinary from it. Because that is exactly what he did.
