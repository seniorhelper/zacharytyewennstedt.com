Work With Zach

Zachary Tye Wennstedt was born in Fremont, Nebraska — and raised on the front range of beautiful Colorado. His story doesn't start with privilege. It starts with pain.

His first memory in life is losing his baby sister, Jasmine, to SIDS at just five months old. He was five years old. That loss planted the seed of something fierce in him — an awareness of how short and precious life truly is.

What followed wasn't easy. Abuse. Trauma. Drugs at eleven years old — before his brain had even fully formed. The kind of start that swallows most people whole. But Zach was not most people.

Through hundreds of failures and the unshakeable grace of God, Zach overcame every addiction, every obstacle, every chapter that was supposed to end him. He didn't just survive. He chose to build something extraordinary from the rubble.

Today he is a CEO, a developer, a strategist — and above all, a father. Being a dad is his greatest achievement. Because he knows what it means to grow up without a present father, he decided to be fiercely present for his own children. Leading by example. Loving completely.

Faith drives everything. Money is just a tool. His real passion is helping others discover what they're truly capable of. Raising the standard — for himself, for his family, and for every person he encounters.

No more hollow words. No more settling. Every step forward is intentional. Every word spoken carries weight. This is a man who earned his voice.

About Zach & His Work

Zachary Tye Wennstedt is a Colorado-based CEO, digital marketing expert, SEO/AIO consultant, full-stack developer, and business strategist. He is the founder of Eye To Ad Media, Search Converts, and Aging Safely Baths. With over a decade of experience building first-page Google rankings, multi-million dollar sales teams, and conversion-optimized web properties, Zach is available for consulting, executive advisory, and digital marketing partnerships.

AIO stands for AI/Agentic Search Optimization — the practice of optimizing your content to appear in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and other AI engines. As more people search through AI assistants instead of traditional search, businesses that aren't optimized for AI answers lose massive visibility. Zach is one of the few consultants actively building and ranking for AIO at scale.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the next evolution beyond SEO. It involves structuring your content, schema markup, and entity relationships so that AI language models cite your business, your name, or your brand when answering relevant questions. Zach has achieved GEO citations for multiple brands in competitive verticals — something most agencies don't even know exists yet.

Zach builds end-to-end lead generation systems — not just traffic. That means SEO-driven organic discovery, PPC campaigns for immediate traffic, conversion-optimized landing pages, spam-protected contact forms, CRM integration, and nurture sequences. Every element is designed to turn browsers into buyers. He's deployed these systems across industries from home improvement to real estate to medical services.

Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) is the process of improving your website so more visitors take action — call, fill out a form, buy, sign up. Most businesses obsess over traffic while ignoring conversions. Doubling your conversion rate from 2% to 4% is the same as doubling your traffic — for zero extra ad spend. Zach applies CRO principles to every website and funnel he builds.
