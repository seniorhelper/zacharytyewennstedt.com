What a web designer should be responsible for

Design that gets found, then gets the call.

Most web designers are judged on how the site looks the day it launches. I'd rather be judged on the phone calls six months later. That means the search structure, the speed, the offer and the one clear action are designed in — not handed to "the SEO guy" afterward.

Found

Page-per-service structure, speed under three seconds on a phone, schema, direct answers, and the AI-search layer — built in, so Google, Maps and ChatGPT can find and cite the site from launch.

Chosen

A headline that names the customer's problem, proof next to the offer, real pricing signals, and photos that match the words. Design that makes a stranger trust you in eight seconds.

Called

The phone number in the first screen, one primary action, a form with five fields, and — if you want it — a sales-trained assistant that books the visit while you're on a job.
