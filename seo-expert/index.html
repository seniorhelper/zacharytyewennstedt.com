What an SEO expert actually does in 2026

Found. Chosen. Called.

Ranking was the whole job in 2015. Today an SEO consultant works three surfaces — Google's organic results, the Google Maps pack, and the AI answers from Google, ChatGPT and Perplexity — and then has to make sure the visit turns into a call. Anyone still selling you "rankings" is selling you a third of the job.

Organic search

Pages built to answer the real question for each service and place, fast, structured, and linked so Google understands which page matters. Not a blog nobody reads.

Google Maps

The map pack sits above everything for local searches and moves fastest. A finished profile, reviews that name the service, service areas that match how customers actually search.

AI answers

ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI cite businesses they can verify: consistent facts, direct answers, structured data, real mentions. I put clients through the directory and press platforms we own so the facts are findable.

How I work

Founder-led. Tested on my own companies first.

Every method I use for a client was used on one of my own businesses first — because I have been burned by agencies too, and I won't sell what I haven't proven. The agency side of that work is Denver SEO at Eye To Ad Media, where campaigns run $500–$3,000 a month quoted to the business; the consulting side is me, personally, when a company needs a second opinion, a strategy, or an in-house team coached.

What I won't do: guarantee rankings (nobody honest can), buy links, publish AI filler, or itemize a method that changes every month. What I will do: show you the live work, tell you the truth about your site, and say "you don't need me yet" when it's true.
