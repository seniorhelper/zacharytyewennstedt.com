    Where your month goes

    The stage with the smallest carry-through is almost never the one people assume. Most owners are convinced they need more visitors when the actual hole is three rows down, between a lead arriving and a human replying to it.

    In order of how much they cost you

    Three things lose more leads
    than everything else combined

    01

    The offer was not worth acting on

    Not the design. The offer. “Contact us for a free consultation” is not an offer, it is a chore with a nicer name. If a visitor cannot say out loud what they get and why now, nothing downstream matters.

    02

    The path had friction in it

    Eleven form fields. A phone number rendered as an image. A page that takes six seconds on a phone in a basement. Every one of these is a small tax, and taxes compound.

    03

    Nobody replied fast enough

    The least glamorous item on the list and the one I fix first. A lead that waits until Monday is competing against the company that answered in five minutes, and it is not close.

    The uncomfortable one

    5 minutes

    That is roughly the window in which an interested person is still thinking about you. After that you are interrupting them. Everything I build — the routing, the chatbots, the agents — exists to get inside that window, because it is the cheapest advantage available to almost every business I meet.
